Steer it. Trim it. Win it.

A 3D sailing race for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, sailed with your own hands on the tiller and the mainsheet.

One thumb steers, the other trims. Pull the sheet in and the sail fills; let it run and it flogs. Underneath is a full sailing simulation: puffs and shifts that march down the course, waves you can surf, a sailor who hikes against the heel, and a boom that slams across in a careless gybe hard enough to put you in the water.

Climb the ladder

Everyone starts in an Opti. Win races to raise your rating and the next boat opens: a single-hander, a sportboat, a keelboat with crew on the rail and a spinnaker, a grand prix racer, and at the top a foiling catamaran that flies at close to thirty knots. Every class sails like itself: dinghies capsize, keelboats broach, the cat lifts onto its foils.

On the water

  • Fleet races against seven computer sailors, on a different course every race, under its own sky: dawn to dusk, now and then rain or sea fog.
  • Match races, one against one.
  • Five challenges: timed start, gybe slalom, rounding sprint, surf run and heavy air. Every timed start is measured: how late, how far from the line, how fast.
  • Real instruments: speed, heel, true and apparent wind, and live standings.
  • Online races for two to eight sailors through Game Center, with a rating and monthly season points.
  • Learn to sail in two minutes with a guided first sail.

On iPhone Duo the race fills both screens: folded for a race in your pocket, open for the big picture.

Free. No ads, no purchases, no account.
